# AI-Assisted-CTI-EPISTEMIC-STATUS-STUDY-
This project investigates whether Large Language Models (LLMs), when used in Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) workflows, transform explicitly unverified cyber-threat claims into statements that communicate a stronger level of certainty or evidentiary credibility.

The research focuses on epistemic-status distortion: a situation in which the evidentiary status of a claim changes during AI-assisted processing without sufficient supporting evidence.

The objective is to examine the reliability of AI-generated threat intelligence and explore the implications for cybersecurity analysts, intelligence assessment and governance.
